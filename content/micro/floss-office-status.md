You blink your eye and there are new FLOSS office projects.

First, LibreOffice is clearly splitting between [Document foundation](https://www.documentfoundation.org/) and [Collabora Online/Desktop](https://collaboraonline.github.io/). See also current discussion on that [here](https://www.collaboraonline.com/blog/tdf-ejects-its-core-developers/) and [here](https://blog.documentfoundation.org/blog/2026/04/01/comment-about-collabora-blog-post/).

In the meantime, OnlyOffice are forked to [Euro-Office](https://nextcloud.com/blog/press_releases/industry-initiative-launches-euro-office-as-true-sovereign-office-suite/). The main reason is to get European digital sovereignty.

Lots of users' confusion ahead soon, I'm afraid. Some people still use and install OpenOffice, even though it's dead effectively.
